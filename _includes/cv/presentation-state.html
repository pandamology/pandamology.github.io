{%- comment -%} Compare ISO dates at the precision actually recorded. A month-only talk is upcoming throughout that month if marked upcoming; after it, it is past. Do not feed partial dates through Liquid's date filter, which fills missing components and can turn a year or month into an invented date. {%- endcomment -%} {%- assign cv_talk_date = include.talk.date | append: '' -%} {%- assign cv_talk_precision = cv_talk_date | size -%} {%- assign cv_build_date = site.cv_as_of | default: site.time -%} {%- assign cv_comparison_date = cv_build_date | date: '%Y-%m-%d' | slice: 0, cv_talk_precision -%} {%- if cv_talk_date > cv_comparison_date -%} upcoming {%- elsif cv_talk_date == cv_comparison_date and include.talk.status == 'upcoming' -%} upcoming {%- else -%} past {%- endif -%}
